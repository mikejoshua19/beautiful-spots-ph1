Top 5 Spots in El Nido

Breathtaking destinations, photos, and video clips you must explore

Big Lagoon

The Big Lagoon is one of El Nido's crown jewels, framed by towering karst cliffs and shallow, tranquil water. It is a premier spot for kayaking and swimming amidst breathtaking scenery.

Secret Lagoon

Hidden behind a wall of majestic dark cliffs on Miniloc Island, the Secret Lagoon is accessed by squeezing through a small opening in the rocks, revealing a peaceful, enclosed pool.

Nacpan Beach

Nacpan Beach is a pristine, four-kilometer expanse of golden sand and swaying coconut palms. Known for its relaxed vibe and rolling waves, it is ideal for beach lounging and sunsets.

Seven Commandos Beach

Named after the seven commandos who once lived on the island, Seven Commandos features powdery white sand, crystal-clear water, and rope swings tied to coastal palm trees.

Las Cabañas Beach

Las Cabañas Beach (Marimegmeg Beach) is famous for its vibrant beachfront bars, exciting zipline adventures between islands, and being a top sunset-watching location.
